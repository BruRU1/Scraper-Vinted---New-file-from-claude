What this is (and isn't)
  • "Group average" is what similar active listings are currently asking, not a proven sale price – Vinted items often sell for less after offers.
  • Profit ignores shipping, Vinted's buyer protection fee, and your own time/effort.
  • Only listings from genuine Vinted category browses are included – brand keyword-search results are excluded here since they catch too much unrelated junk to safely act on.
  • Score blends how underpriced an item is (70%) with how quickly its group has historically sold (30%) – it's a ranking, not a currency amount.
