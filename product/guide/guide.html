1. Opening the file

Excel: open the .xlsx and enable editing. Google Sheets: upload to Google Drive, right-click → Open with → Google Sheets (or File → Import). No macros are used, so everything works in both.

2. The sheets

3. Step by step

  1. Check the tax year start date on Settings (default 6 April 2026).
  2. Enter each payment on Income: date, customer, description, amount. The quarter fills in automatically.
  3. Enter each cost on Expenses, choosing the category from the drop-down.
  4. Open Quarterly Summary after each quarter ends and use the totals for your Making Tax Digital quarterly update.
  5. Open Tax Estimate at any time to see roughly what to set aside.

5. How the estimate is calculated

  • Taxable profit = income minus expenses, or minus the £1,000 trading allowance if that is higher.
  • Personal allowance applies in full up to £100,000 of profit, reducing by £1 for every £2 above that.
  • Income Tax at 20% / 40% / 45% using the bands on the Settings sheet.
  • Class 4 NIC at 6% between the lower and upper profits limits and 2% above.
Assumptions: your only income is this self-employment; England, Wales and Northern Ireland rates (Scottish income tax rates differ); no student loan, pension or other reliefs; no payments on account.
Important: this workbook gives estimates to help you plan. It is not tax advice and does not submit anything to HMRC. Always check current rates on GOV.UK and consult an accountant if unsure. Rates are editable on the Settings sheet.

6. Tips

  • Only type in the yellow cells; formulas in other cells should not be overwritten.
  • Keep receipts – this tracker records figures, it does not replace evidence.
  • Save a fresh copy of the file each tax year and change the start date on Settings.
